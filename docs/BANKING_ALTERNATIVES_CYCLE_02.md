The evaluator does not predict captured cells, opponent reactions, settlement or a win.

## Proposal after this result

1. **Do not promote this candidate.** It cannot act in either examined loss, and even a financially feasible attack would not establish tactical value. Do not lower the 1.5 threshold or contact coefficients against these same two losses to manufacture an opportunity.
2. Prioritize a **pre-contact capital experiment**, because the measured deficit predates the terminal banking hold. Freeze opening schedules/contact reserve variants on development matches; log first-contact time, bank/land, adjacent bank ratios, tax, committed force, growth and newly exposed contacts. Preserve no-contact baseline behavior and exact native reserve/minimum-command constraints.
3. Compare opening conservation with its real cost: delayed land/income and possible encirclement. Static contact counts cannot forecast new contact growth; use the existing exact ownership replay for geometry-dependent claims. Do not infer geometry from counts.
4. Enumerate alternatives **before** the empire is already shrinking; retain direction-verified attack force and all physical actors. An explicitly designed defense-wall exception is a separate policy experiment requiring a valid outcome model, not merely changing the decision label to PRESSURE.
5. Collect the planned **16 development + 8 untouched held-out** representative matches, with development-only selection and category coverage. This cycle does not satisfy that quota or the larger dynamic-frontier research milestone. Only then consider a separately authorized, frozen, single-player baseline/candidate treatment with active-policy proof and whole-match uncertainty.
